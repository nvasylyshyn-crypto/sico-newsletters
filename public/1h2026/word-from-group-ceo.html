A Word from the Group CEO

During times of uncertainty, what matters most is how we support one another and remain focused on what truly matters — the wellbeing of our people and the trust placed in us by our clients and stakeholders.

At SICO, our priority continues to be safeguarding the interests of our employees, clients, and partners. Our teams remain closely engaged with clients across our markets, proactively communicating our views on market developments, providing updates on portfolio performance, and offering guidance and our insights on how to navigate the evolving environment.

While periods of heightened volatility in financial markets can present challenges, they can also create opportunities. Guided by our disciplined investment approach and deep market expertise, we remain focused on helping our clients identify long-term value and make informed decisions.

Above all, we remain grateful for the dedication of our employees and the continued confidence of our clients and stakeholders. Together, we will continue to move forward with resilience and unity.

Thank you for your continued trust.

Najla Al Shirawi

Group Chief Executive Officer
