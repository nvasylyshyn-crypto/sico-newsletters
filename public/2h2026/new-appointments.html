Group Deputy Chief Executive Officer for Sell Side Business

Mishal Alhellow

Group Chief Innovation Officer

Mishal brings over 25 years of experience in the technology sector across both public and private domains. Prior to this appointment, he served as Chief Operating Officer of SICO Capital in Riyadh. Earlier in his career, he held key roles at Arcapita and the Ministry of Finance and National Economy in Bahrain.

He has also contributed to academia and industry development, having served as a Council Member of the College of Information Technology at the University of Bahrain. He is the Founder and Chairman of Bahrain’s Technology and Business Association.

Mishal holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the Malaysian Open University and a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from the University of Bahrain.
